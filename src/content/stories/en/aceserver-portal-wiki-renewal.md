We renewed the Aceserver Portal and Aceserver WIKI. Aceserver is a free public Minecraft server that anyone can join from either Java Edition or Bedrock Edition. We reviewed the roles of the two sites and how easily people can move between them, so first-time visitors can understand the path to joining and active players can find the information they need.

## The Portal Makes the First Step Clearer

The portal is the entry point for getting to know Aceserver. From the homepage, you can go to the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/acsv), which is the starting point for joining; the [Aceserver WIKI](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/), where you can check rules and details; videos that show the atmosphere of play; and [world maps](/en/world-map/) for each world.

If this is your first visit, start by looking over the [Aceserver Portal](/en/) and checking the guidance on the official Discord.

## The WIKI Makes Things Easier to Find

The WIKI organizes the information players may need while playing into categories, including how to join, rules and ban conditions, Discord linking, commands, and plugins. It also provides site search to make relevant articles easier to reach.

Rules and specifications can change, so always check the WIKI for the latest information.

## Move Between the Two Sites for What You Need

The portal is the place to start when you want to join or see which worlds are available, while the WIKI is the place to check a rule or command. We made it possible to move between both sites and avoid duplicating detailed explanations on the portal.

Open the [Aceserver Portal](/en/) when you want to learn about joining or the server atmosphere, the [Aceserver WIKI](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/) when you want details about rules or play, and the [world maps](/en/world-map/) when you want to see the worlds.

## We Will Keep Making Information Easier to Reach

We will continue improving guidance and articles on the portal and WIKI after publication. If there is something you would like to see added, please let us know on the official Discord.
