From the early Wiki to planning events and linking chats, these are some of the contributions to Aceserver documented in official Discord announcements, event reports, and records from earlier channels.

## The early days

In December 2020, てぃあ coordinated early railway construction and explained the application process builders were asked to follow at the time. [Railway construction announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/783543829676556349)

In December 2020, てぃあ announced the launch of an early Wiki containing the rules. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/784929714296193034)

In January 2021, Gui introduced two groups. The Aceserver Tourism Association, led by KotaMizuno, built places to visit, while Master_Yinfe’s Between Tower focused on pixel art. The announcement also named 6nuts, \_Dont_Stop\_, none_violet, taisa68, and Flare1011 as early participants. [Community group announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/798378478293614643)

In Between Tower’s old Discord reports, PillowKebap announced the completion of the first pixel artwork, “Beekeeper,” in January 2021 and a second work of about 65,000 blocks in August. PillowKebap thanked the people who gathered and placed materials. dont_stop reported on the third project on the group’s behalf. In 2022, izaiza contributed clay blocks, and in March naop5 and Ivalaidea became official members in recognition of their contributions. In April, PillowKebap announced the opening of a map art display at the museum.

In August 2021, Gui said the server began so anyone could visit はっ and play in their world. Gui also announced that コタ would take over operational decisions for the time being. コタ confirmed accepting the role. [Gui’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880745650009047080) [コタ’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880760188351705178)

In August 2021, KOTA-SAN previewed a call for first-anniversary logo designs. In September, Mugi reported reorganizing Discord and opened submissions. In November, てぃあ announced that YM’s design had been selected and applied on Discord. [Call preview](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/881193662321684601) [Submissions open](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/882433940621758474) [Selected logo announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/905830523761487872)

## Promotion and events

In May 2022, キツネコ announced an alternative boat race using a course built by Hako_Otoko. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/970291030152847390)

In November 2022, yanyan_fafnir reported that ゆり (Yū) had become a supporter after a recommendation from Sosuke. [Supporter appointment announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1043527218191663114)

In December 2022, Yukke opened a channel for the Aceserver Photography Club. Records from the old Discord channel show that taibon0727 asked for photos of old and new worlds for an album. By January 2023, dont_stop had shared a photo commemorating an ascent of Saihate, PillowKebap had contributed from their saved photos, and nnn_percent had shared photos of builds.

In March 2023, ゆり invited people to join filming for a server introduction video and shared the order for filming towns and worlds. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1087641197947932704)

In May 2023, ゆり shared the publicity team’s plans to start running the YouTube and Twitter accounts on a regular basis. [Publicity plans](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1110469768420532235)

タケ made the poster for Ace Summer Festival in 2024. For the year-end “つなぐASF” event, taibon helped with the New Year countdown device. タケ reported the end of the full program in January 2025. [Poster announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1274157736015429744) [Countdown announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1319967701808189493) [Closing report](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1325487116419076129)

In January 2025, Aceserver announced that Wenti (Hosttech255), Pluto, and あすたん had become event supporters. [Event supporter announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1323911696237990039)

The 2025 summer festival announcement named センチュリーズ for preparing and providing the fireworks display. The post-event thanks named セガ, リンゴポン, あまぎ, 新人, ジェスター, ホステッチ and イザイザ for the fireworks, and コッカー and sh for overall coordination. エント worked on the circuit, and moon and セキセイ on golf. ハルビィ, セキセイ and sh worked on survival PvP, and はっ and sh on the special event. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1408390549370572880) [Post-event thanks](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/779569416438415421/1411394624026710166)

In October 2025, sh thanked the people who helped make events for the Aceserver Revitalization Committee and invited ideas for the next one. A November announcement named amagi_467 as the initiator of the first Aceserver Mahjong Tournament, which the committee planned to host. [Event thanks and call for ideas](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1432028687376715897) [Mahjong tournament announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1442742157315149865)

For the event at the end of 2025, ハルビィ, セキセイ and タケ helped with the snowy mountain race. セキセイ developed the snowball fight PvP plugin. 山田 built the venue and ゆり supported live commentary and event operations. Participants also helped with the rehearsal. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1454846975227854859)

A December 2025 announcement explained how players could join a planned photo shoot for a new header image at Tōgenkyō Shrine. [Photo shoot invitation](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1455820341988298905)

In August 2026, sekisei and ふーや announced a jointly hosted online CTF treasure hunt where players solve puzzles such as ciphers to find flags. Aceserver CTF2026 began on August 28. [Co-host announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1541785510094184449) [Opening report](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/779569416438415421/1542704904055824394)

## Building and joint projects

In June 2022, yanyan_fafnir explained how to enter the floating-island contest that led to work on the former lobby and how entries would be presented at judging. The announcement named はっ and members with the supporter role or above as judges. [Contest entry rules](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/982990352422162482) [Judging session announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/986294242848669756)

In July 2022, Maro led construction of the former lobby and reported its completion. The build logs from the old Discord channel thanked BM_yurikun for landscaping and _BE_ChalkyMink87 for embedding ore in the foundation. KOTA-SAN set up a place to share updates on the build.

From October 2022 to January 2023, players worked on draining an ocean monument and building a guardian facility while also excavating for a wither skeleton facility. According to the old Discord work records, taka8621 opened a project channel. Moon_666 organized the drainage and excavation, replenished materials, and worked in an assigned area. Yukke called for help with the excavation and prepared a route to the fortress.

taibon0727 provided materials including glass, a place for travel boats, and equipment for working with sand. SPdari supplied sand. UtaTheIrasutā worked on filling in lava and set up a potion brewing station. nnn_percent helped with sand for filling, and あすたん helped excavate. SPdari also thanked Buraki for showing people how to help.

At the end of December 2022, edamameRex_java reported that construction of the hot-spring inn had finished, according to the original project records.

In November 2025, End&#12539;コッカー reported installing gates in eight directions to reach distant parts of the survival server. [Gate installation announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1434193495752704134)

## Technology and support

According to old Discord records, in August 2021 KOTA-SAN reported creating and launching Ace-kun, a bot that automatically sent instructions to newcomers in the lobby. In January 2022, KOTA-SAN reported restoring Ace-kun as one of the co-creators of Ace-kun and Kutya. In May, KOTA-SAN added automatic posting of a self-introduction template.

In June 2022, Mugi updated the text-to-speech bot Kutya to support chat inside voice channels. In March 2023, Mugi announced a bot for sending anonymous feedback to the server team. [Kutya update](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/986439863937155122) [Anonymous feedback announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1087976366126927882)

In July 2023, Yukke announced creating a new resource server world. [Resource world announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1135574163080364103)

In September 2023, Aceserver announced that あすたん had joined the operations team and that Taka would oversee systems. In May 2024, はっ announced that sh and sekisei had taken on user support roles covering systems. [2023 appointments](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1151168895076548669) [2024 support roles](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1236592686253867008)

In November 2025, a Minecraft and Discord chat integration made by あすたん was introduced. [Chat integration announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1437973211961622670)

In February 2026, Merutak reported creating two Discord roles for notices about trouble and server outages. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1477293143773810822)

In July 2026, sekisei announced the first technical talk about servers and the Internet. [Technical talk announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1532641413500633108)

These activities are only some of the contributions people have made. Thank you to everyone who has helped.
