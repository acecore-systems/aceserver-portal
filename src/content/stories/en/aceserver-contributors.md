Many people have supported Aceserver in different ways. Here are some of their contributions from official Discord announcements. Joining the official Discord is required to view the original posts.

## The early days

In December 2020, てぃあ announced the launch of an early Wiki containing the rules. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/784929714296193034)

In August 2021, Gui said the server began so anyone could visit はっ (はっくん, ハット卿) and play in their world. Gui also announced that コタ would take over operational decisions for the time being. コタ confirmed accepting the role. [Gui’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880745650009047080) [コタ’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880760188351705178)

## Promotion and events

In May 2022, キツネコ announced an alternative boat race using a course built by Hako_Otoko. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/970291030152847390)

In March 2023, ゆり invited people to join filming for a server introduction video and shared the order for filming towns and worlds. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1087641197947932704)

タケ made the poster for Ace Summer Festival in 2024. For the year-end “つなぐASF” event, taibon helped with the New Year countdown device. タケ reported the end of the full program in January 2025. [Poster announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1274157736015429744) [Countdown announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1319967701808189493) [Closing report](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1325487116419076129)

The 2025 summer festival announcement named センチュリーズ for preparing and providing the fireworks display. The post-event thanks named セガ, リンゴポン, あまぎ, 新人, ジェスター, ホステッチ and イザイザ for the fireworks, and コッカー and sh for overall coordination. エント worked on the circuit, and moon and セキセイ on golf. ハルビィ, セキセイ and sh worked on survival PvP, and はっ and sh on the special event. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1408390549370572880) [Post-event thanks](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/779569416438415421/1411394624026710166)

For the event at the end of 2025, ハルビィ, セキセイ and タケ helped with the snowy mountain race. セキセイ developed the snowball fight PvP plugin. 山田 built the venue and ゆり supported live commentary and event operations. Participants also helped with the rehearsal. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1454846975227854859)

In August 2026, sekisei and ふーや announced a jointly hosted online CTF treasure hunt where players solve puzzles such as ciphers to find flags. Aceserver CTF2026 began on August 28. [Co-host announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1541785510094184449) [Opening report](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/779569416438415421/1542704904055824394)

## Technology and support

In July 2023, Yukke announced creating a new resource server world. In November 2025, a Minecraft and Discord chat integration made by あすたん (asutarisucu) was introduced. [Resource world announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1135574163080364103) [Chat integration announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1437973211961622670)

In February 2026, Merutak reported creating two Discord roles for notices about trouble and server outages. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/1477293143773810822)

These activities are only some of the contributions people have made. Thank you to everyone who has helped.
