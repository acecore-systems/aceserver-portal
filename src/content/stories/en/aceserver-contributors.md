From the early Wiki to planning events and linking chats, these are some of the contributions to Aceserver documented in official Discord announcements, event reports, and records from earlier channels.

## The early days

In November 2020, てぃあ proposed a themed home-building contest with voting at a venue laid out in plots. In December, てぃあ reported creating a channel for railway construction applications.

On November 19, 2020, Mugi pointed out that without nighttime, villagers could not sleep, which would stop iron golem farms from working. Mugi also warned that villagers at trading halls would stop restocking. Gui acknowledged the report. The next day, when a player asked how the server had been set up, Gui explained that multiplayer was hosted on a rented server.

In December 2020, てぃあ coordinated early railway construction and explained the application process builders were asked to follow at the time. [Railway construction announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/783543829676556349)

In December 2020, てぃあ announced the launch of an early Wiki containing the rules. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/784929714296193034)

In January 2021, Gui introduced two groups. The Aceserver Tourism Association, led by KotaMizuno, built places to visit, while Master_Yinfe’s Between Tower focused on pixel art. The announcement also named 6nuts, \_Dont_Stop\_, none_violet, taisa68, and Flare1011 as early participants. [Community group announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/798378478293614643)

In Between Tower’s old Discord reports, PillowKebap announced the completion of the first pixel artwork, “Beekeeper,” in January 2021 and a second work of about 65,000 blocks in August. PillowKebap thanked the people who gathered and placed materials. dont_stop reported on the third project on the group’s behalf. In 2022, izaiza contributed clay blocks, and in March naop5 and Ivalaidea became official members in recognition of their contributions. In April, PillowKebap announced the opening of a map art display at the museum.

In August 2021, Gui said the server began so anyone could visit はっ and play in their world. Gui also announced that コタ would take over operational decisions for the time being. コタ confirmed accepting the role. [Gui’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880745650009047080) [コタ’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880760188351705178)

In August 2021, KOTA-SAN previewed a call for first-anniversary logo designs. In September, Mugi reported reorganizing Discord and opened submissions. On October 2, Mugi opened voting on five candidate designs. On November 4, てぃあ announced that YM’s design had been selected and applied on Discord, and thanked YM, J&#27663;, Mea, and Café Mocha for their work. [Call preview](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/881193662321684601) [Submissions open](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/882433940621758474) [Voting announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/882218927055331369/893689014039765053) [Selection and thanks to the designers](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/882218927055331369/905494118606725131) [Selected logo announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/905830523761487872)

On August 30, 2021, &#38646; asked for players who could take pandas and foxes home in the game on their own. KOTA-SAN backed the call on behalf of the server staff. [&#38646;’s call for adopters](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/881827499074539611/882286204320624671) [KOTA-SAN’s call](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/881827499074539611/882286206757535826)

On May 8, 2021, KOTA-SAN created a dedicated category for questions about Discord and offered to answer them. On October 8, てぃあ announced the opening of the “Haiku and Senryū Room.”
