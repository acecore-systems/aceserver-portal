From the early Wiki to planning events and linking chats, these are some of the contributions to Aceserver documented in official Discord announcements, event reports, and records from earlier channels.

## The early days

In December 2020, てぃあ coordinated early railway construction and explained the application process builders were asked to follow at the time. [Railway construction announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/783543829676556349)

In December 2020, てぃあ announced the launch of an early Wiki containing the rules. [Original post](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/784929714296193034)

In January 2021, Gui introduced two groups. The Aceserver Tourism Association, led by KotaMizuno, built places to visit, while Master_Yinfe’s Between Tower focused on pixel art. The announcement also named 6nuts, \_Dont_Stop\_, none_violet, taisa68, and Flare1011 as early participants. [Community group announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/798378478293614643)

In Between Tower’s old Discord reports, PillowKebap announced the completion of the first pixel artwork, “Beekeeper,” in January 2021 and a second work of about 65,000 blocks in August. PillowKebap thanked the people who gathered and placed materials. dont_stop reported on the third project on the group’s behalf. In 2022, izaiza contributed clay blocks, and in March naop5 and Ivalaidea became official members in recognition of their contributions. In April, PillowKebap announced the opening of a map art display at the museum.

In August 2021, Gui said the server began so anyone could visit はっ and play in their world. Gui also announced that コタ would take over operational decisions for the time being. コタ confirmed accepting the role. [Gui’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880745650009047080) [コタ’s announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/880760188351705178)

In August 2021, KOTA-SAN previewed a call for first-anniversary logo designs. In September, Mugi reported reorganizing Discord and opened submissions. On October 2, Mugi opened voting on five candidate designs. On November 4, てぃあ announced that YM’s design had been selected and applied on Discord, and thanked YM, J&#27663;, Mea, and Café Mocha for their work. [Call preview](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/881193662321684601) [Submissions open](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/882433940621758474) [Voting announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/882218927055331369/893689014039765053) [Selection and thanks to the designers](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/882218927055331369/905494118606725131) [Selected logo announcement](https://discord.com/channels/737538781024092170/774152638450237460/905830523761487872)

## Communities and creative groups

In December 2020, Gui opened an activity channel for the Ace Railway Company, and oisiisyouyu invited people to join. In March 2021, oisiisyouyu announced that the group would focus on the subway.

In an introduction posted to the old Discord by KOTA-SAN, the Aceserver Tourism Association listed MKOTA_SAN as its leader at the time. The introduction described the opening of a temple, an aquarium, and a casino district with facilities including a hotel and an art museum. It also covered contests and the joint development of the association’s bot Kutya. In March 2021, KOTA-SAN unveiled a slot machine in the association’s casino district and reported adding prizes the following day.

In March 2021, kuroneko229 introduced the Public Works Department, naming Kuro neko2229 as its leader at the time. In November 2022, kuroneko229 reported what the group, by then the Private Works Department, had worked on, including a subway, Nether roads, streetlights, a shopping district, and apartments.

In December 2020, Mugi built the RS Circuit Engineering Club’s clubhouse and reported its completion. Mugi introduced the RS Circuit Engineering Club’s work and its leader, sousuke3018. Introductions to the SEGA Empire named segarikihiro as its leader at the time.

The Amusement Park Group’s leader, kurenohachi (Hachi), reported choosing a site for the planned build with help from others. In a post about the Kuwa Kingdom, Yukke listed yukke40901 as its leader and invited players to help build its castle and capital. Nishikino Sega said that the Eirin-tai group would support the Kuwa Kingdom’s work.

An introduction to the Ancient City Restoration Group named Golden Cocker as its leader at the time and Taibon and タケ as early participants. A call from the RPG Creation Group also listed Golden Cocker as leader and invited people to help make RPG-style towns and stories.

As owner of A Yū (A-shō), ゆり (Yū) described the sale of materials and called for volunteers to build a play area.

In 2022, s1nan0sub shared the rules for using Iroha Village and reported that Inaba Village’s shared storage facility was complete.

In July 2023, ゆり introduced an alliance between the Village of the Moon God and the RPG Creation Group in an Ace Lab announcement. It named BM\_yurikun and \_BE\_golden\_cokka as the leaders at the time, and amya7238 and \_BE\_golden\_cokka as early members. The activities outlined included ACE1GP ice boat racing and RPG creation.

In September 2023, タケ introduced the Ace Monorail Take Company, listing Take\_no\_niwa as leader and sekisei0077 and HeroTree as early members. The introduction also covered how the group operated and how to contact it.

In October 2023, PetiteShelf7874 introduced the activities and map of Small Hill Village. The introduction listed PetiteShelf7874 as leader and yamaharu929, kacchan0706, bananakun315, and TK7K2 as early members.

In September 2024, sekisei introduced Mars (working title), a group for planning and making minigames. The introduction listed sekisei0077 as leader and usualparrot and miin2000 as early members. sekisei described minigame ideas based on falling and Elytra and invited people to help build and develop them.
