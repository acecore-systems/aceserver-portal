When you visit Aceserver for the first time, it can be hard to know where to look first. This guide gives you a simple order for using the official portal, world maps, videos, WIKI, and connection help.

If you are not sure where to find guidance, use the portal’s site search with terms such as “how to join” or “world map.” Open the page you find and check the latest joining requirements and rules.

Connection destinations, ports, device-specific steps including Switch, joining requirements, and rules can change. This article is an introduction, so always treat the latest official guidance as the authority before you join.

## The first four official places to open

1. Check the [official Aceserver portal](/en/) for the main entry points.
2. Join the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/acsv) and check the latest joining and maintenance notices.
3. Read the current joining steps in the [Aceserver WIKI joining guide](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/in/).
4. Read the [Aceserver WIKI rules index](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/rules-index/) before entering the game.

Following this order takes you to information for the current Aceserver instead of relying on an old article or an unofficial connection method. If anything is unclear, do not guess; ask through the official Discord.

## Use the portal to find where to go next

The portal is the signpost for people meeting Aceserver for the first time. It brings together the fact that players can join from Java Edition and Bedrock Edition, plus entry points for the official Discord, WIKI, world maps, and videos.

For the conditions that let Java and Bedrock players play together and the points for recognizing a compatible server, see [Can Java and Bedrock Play Together?](/en/stories/minecraft-java-bedrock-crossplay/).

Start from the home page, then choose the page that matches your goal.

| What you want to do                    | Where to open    | What to check first                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find the latest joining news           | Official Discord | Maintenance, joining requirements, and connection notices |
| See what places exist                  | World maps       | The world you want to see and map availability            |
| Get a feel for the community or events | Videos           | Recent official channel videos and public meetings        |
| Look up rules or gameplay              | WIKI             | Joining steps, rules, and guides for each world           |

The portal is the entrance to current information, not a place to memorize connection details. Check the joining guide and Discord notices once more immediately before connecting.

## Find a place you want to explore with world maps

The [world maps](/en/world-map/) link to published maps such as the Main World, Resource World, RPG, Lobby, Season worlds, and event maps. If you want to tour builds, collect resources, or play RPG, learning a world's name from the map makes it easier to find the related WIKI and Discord guidance.

A map being displayed does not by itself guarantee that you can enter it right now or use a particular feature. World status and travel methods can change, so check the latest announcements and rules before acting.

## Choose a server by what you want to do

For building and social play, start with the Life server. Visit the Resource server to gather materials, or a Season server to explore a world for a particular version. For minigames, look at the Content servers. If you want to propose a longer-running project, read the Concept server guidance. The [WIKI server guide](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/other-server/) links to each server's purpose and rules. Check Discord for which servers are currently available.

For shops and currency, see the [WIKI currency and My Page guide](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/currency-web/). Check the Discord bulletin board before using prices or other conditions that may change.

## Use videos to understand the server atmosphere

The [official video page](/en/youtube-search-aceserver/) shows recent videos such as event streams, world introductions, and public meetings. It is useful when you want to understand buildings, events, and the community atmosphere that text alone cannot show.

Videos are a doorway to understanding the community. Do not assume that a connection destination, feature, or rule shown in an older video is current. Confirm joining steps and rules on the current official Discord and WIKI pages.

## Use the WIKI to confirm joining steps and rules

Use the [Aceserver WIKI joining guide](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/in/) for the steps, then choose the community, Minecraft, server, and Discord rules from the [rules index](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net/article/rules-index/). The official Discord posts are the source for rules. If the WIKI and Discord differ, check Discord and ask staff when unsure.

## Join for the first time in this order

1. Use the official portal to confirm Java and Bedrock support and find each official entry point.
2. Join the official Discord and check the newest notices before connecting.
3. Read the WIKI joining guide and rules.
4. Use only the connection method officially shown for your edition and device.
5. Once you enter, read nearby guidance and start with one small goal.

Do not choose an address, port, DNS change, or Switch procedure from an old screenshot or video alone. Do not force an unofficial workaround; ask when the official information is unclear.

If you first want to sort out multiplayer choices for Java, Bedrock, Switch, and mobile, see [How to Play Minecraft with Friends](/en/stories/minecraft-play-with-friends/). For actually joining Aceserver, prioritize the official Discord and WIKI guidance in this article.

## Separate common connection problems from Aceserver guidance

Unable to join can have many causes: your game edition, version, Microsoft account, network, or the server's current guidance. First use [What to Check When You Cannot Join a Minecraft Server](/en/stories/minecraft-server-cannot-join/) for general troubleshooting that applies across devices.

Then check the latest official Discord and WIKI information for Aceserver. Connection destinations, ports, Switch joining steps, maintenance, and requirements must come from official guidance, not a general explanation.

![A block-style connection check that reviews game edition, version, account, and network before returning to official guidance](/uploads/stories/aceserver-beginners-guide-connection-check.png)

When asking for help, it is useful to include:

- Whether you use Java or Bedrock, and which device you use
- Your Minecraft version
- The error you saw and the time you tried to connect
- What you have already checked

You never need to share a password, authentication code, or personal account information.

## Enjoy your first hour

After you get in, you do not need to start a large build or device right away. Read the spawn-area guidance, choose a place that interests you from the world maps, check the needed WIKI rules, and set one small goal.

For example, tour a town, gather a few resources, look for a possible base location, or find a place you saw in a video on the map. Bookmark the official portal, Discord, and WIKI so you can return to reliable information whenever you need it.

Before or after playing, you can read dated records in [Alpha-kun’s picture diary](/en/stories/alpha-diary-guide/). Choose a date that interests you and explore at your own pace.

## Summary

For a first visit to Aceserver, start with the official portal, check the newest news on the official Discord, then read the WIKI joining guide and rules before entering. Use world maps and videos to find places you want to explore and to understand the server atmosphere.

Connection information and rules can change. Give priority to the current official Discord and WIKI guidance over this article, and take your first step safely.
