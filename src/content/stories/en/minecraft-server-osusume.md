When you search for a “recommended Minecraft server,” a ranking or a short introduction can seem enough. But whether you will keep enjoying it depends on what you want to play, whether the joining process is clear, and whether the rules and atmosphere work for you.

This article is published by the official Aceserver portal for people looking for a public server. Rather than making a ranking, it introduces points to check when choosing a server and an indication of who Aceserver may suit.

## Why a ranking alone is not enough

Popularity or a large player count can help narrow the first candidates. However, it can still be hard to stay if the edition you play or the style of play does not fit. Check these six points before you join.

1. **Supported editions**: Check whether you play Java Edition or Bedrock Edition and whether you can join the same server as your friends.
2. **Style of play**: See whether the server's direction matches what you enjoy, such as building, adventure, an economy, or minigames.
3. **A clear path to join**: Joining is easier for first-time players when the steps, required settings, and first guidance are organized.
4. **Rules and community**: It is reassuring to be able to check prohibited actions and the community's priorities in the [official WIKI](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net) before joining.
5. **Finding the information you need**: Look for an easy way to reach information about worlds, events, and ways to play.
6. **An approachable first step**: Knowing where to ask questions and where the guidance begins can reduce uncertainty before joining.

## Who Aceserver may suit

Aceserver is a free public Minecraft server that anyone can join from either Java Edition or Bedrock Edition. It may suit people who want a near-vanilla, largely unrestricted way to play, or who want to check the guidance through Discord before joining.

This page is guidance from the official portal. It does not compare Aceserver against other servers; use the points above and your own preferred way to play when deciding.

## Three steps to join

1. [Check the server overview on the portal](/en/)
2. [Join the official Discord](https://discord.gg/acsv)
3. [Read the WIKI for rules and information to check before joining](https://asv-wiki.acecore.net)

Joining requirements and rules can change, so check the latest guidance on the official Discord and WIKI before you join.

## If you are unsure, start with the joining guide

The surest way to decide whether it suits you is to look at the actual guidance and the community's entry point. After confirming whether you play Java Edition or Bedrock Edition, start by [checking Aceserver's joining guide](/en/).
