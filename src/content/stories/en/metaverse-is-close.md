This is the author’s March 2023 view of Minecraft communities as a metaverse. Before buying VR equipment, consider the experience of building with friends in one world or exploring a server’s town. Minecraft’s official [guide to playing on a server](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/how-play-minecraft-server) explains the multiplayer basics; it is an operating guide, not a definition of the metaverse.

When you hear “metaverse,” you may picture putting on a VR headset and entering a virtual world. Facebook changed its name to Meta and has been focusing on Horizon Worlds, but many people may honestly feel that the idea still does not quite resonate.

## A Huge Metaverse Already Exists

Look at it from another angle and you realize that a huge metaverse already exists: Minecraft.

Players build, explore, and interact with other players in a virtual space. They can take part from a computer or smartphone without a VR headset.

I think the entrance to the metaverse is not a particular device, but a virtual space where people gather.

## The Potential of Minecraft Communities

Running a Minecraft server also means nurturing a small virtual space. On Aceserver, players interact, build together, and enjoy events.

The metaverse is not something only large corporations can create; it already exists as an extension of communities. We hope to find even more interesting ways to use these familiar spaces.
